## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a refined boutique aesthetic tailored for curated stationery, devotional journals, and gift items. It fuses comforting warmth with contemporary editorial restraint. The visual language balances tender charm ("cute") with bespoke maturity ("editorial premium"), entirely avoiding juvenile excess, garish novelty tones, or rigid corporate coldness. 

The target audience seeks meaningful gifts, mindful journaling, and aesthetic desktop pieces that inspire tranquility, faith, and intentionality. The emotional atmosphere is calm, soft-spoken, and warmly welcoming, evoking the tactile feel of creamy high-gsm paper, silk ribbons, and watercolor botanicals.

The core visual movement is **Soft Editorial Minimalism with Tactile Warmth**:
- Generous breathing room (high negative space) allowing each curated piece to stand out as a keepsake.
- Organic, pill-soft contours derived from delicate floral petals and plush textures.
- Subdued, layered pastel surfaces paired with crisp typography to anchor the soft hues in legibility and poise.

## Colors

The color palette centers on dreamy lilac-lavender and delicate rose-petal pinks, supported by creamy warm highlights and deep floral charcoal neutrals for superior contrast and readability.

- **Primary (`#9A80BD` / Tint `#BCA5D8`):** The signature lavender. Represents gentle luxury, creative calm, and artisanal identity. Used for focal call-to-actions, category banners, badges, and primary interactive highlights.
- **Secondary (`#EAA2B1` / Tint `#F4B6C2`):** Soft petal blush. Provides warm affection, gifting delight, and subtle interactive states (hover accents, heart/wishlist toggles, ribbon tags).
- **Tertiary (`#F8EDE3`):** Whipped cream/vanilla paper tone. Serves as surface layering, card backgrounds, and pill container fills, preventing pure cold starkness.
- **Neutral (`#4A3E4E`):** Mulberry-tinged slate charcoal. Replaces harsh blacks with a warm, editorial depth for typography, borders, and crisp iconography.
- **Canvas (`#FDFBF9`):** An airy off-white background with a hint of warm chalk that creates a comfortable, glare-free shopping canvas.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the soft, sculpted terminals of **Quicksand** with the crisp, modern legibility of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Quicksand** is reserved for titles, editorial headers, price displays, and hero declarations. Its rounded geometry reinforces the plush softness of the brand mark without drifting into comic exaggeration.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** anchors product details, long descriptions, e-commerce checkout forms, meta labels, and UI controls. Its balanced proportions and open apertures keep the boutique experience structured, polished, and effortless to read.
- For script flourishes or verses present on physical paper products, preserve them as isolated photographic elements or subtle ambient accents rather than standard UI text styles.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a calm, airy, 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to display craft goods with the care of an independent art studio.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** Max container width of 1240px with `margin: 3rem` and `gutter: 1.5rem`. Wide padding allows product photography to breathe against off-white backdrop layers.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `margin: 2rem` and `gutter: 1.25rem`. Product grids collapse gracefully into 2-up or 3-up arrangements.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `margin-sm: 1.25rem` and `gutter-sm: 1rem`. Generous vertical spacing (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) is maintained between modules to preserve the relaxed pacing.

Negative space is a core material in this design system. Cramped layouts, border-heavy containers, and crowded listing rows are strictly prohibited.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **ambient tint shadows** and **tonal card surfaces** rather than high-contrast structural borders:

- **Surface Tones:** Base pages sit on `#FDFBF9`. Product cards, panels, and side-drawers rest on pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or pale cream (`#F8EDE3`), forming natural, low-strain separation.
- **Shadow Quality:** Shadows are diffused, long-range, and tinted with deep lavender-slate (`rgba(74, 62, 78, 0.06)` up to `rgba(74, 62, 78, 0.12)` for popovers). They emulate warm, soft daylight falling on matte paper stock.
- **Micro-borders:** Where structural boundaries are required for accessibility, utilize ultra-soft borders colored with a low-opacity lavender wash (`#BCA5D8` at 25% opacity) instead of dark dividers.

## Shapes

The design system embraces a **Pill-shaped (`level 3`)** contour aesthetic, directly mirroring the cloud-like, friendly lines of the mascot and floral petal silhouettes.

- **Buttons & Tags:** Full pill radius (`9999px`) to create smooth, welcoming touch points.
- **Cards & Visual Containers:** Outer radius set to `1.5rem` (24px) up to `2rem` (32px), creating soft pocket frames for photos and gift sets.
- **Thumbnails & Badges:** Rounded by `0.75rem` (12px) to keep smaller components harmonious with the broader circular identity.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled in `#9A80BD` with crisp white text (`#FFFFFF`) and a full pill radius. On hover, deepens slightly to `#896EA8` with a soft lavender glow (`0 8px 18px rgba(154, 128, 189, 0.28)`).
- **Secondary / Soft:** Creamy blush background (`#F4B6C2` at 25% opacity) with `#4A3E4E` text. On hover, smooth transition to solid `#F4B6C2`.
- **Outline / Ghost:** Transparent base with a 1.5px border in `#BCA5D8` and text in `#4A3E4E`. Hover fills with `#F8EDE3`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Curated Category Chips:** Full pill shape, padding `0.5rem 1.25rem`. Inactive state uses white fill with a subtle lavender hairline; active state adopts `#9A80BD` with pure white text.
- **Tag Badges (e.g., "Novedad", "Edición Limitada"):** Soft petal-pink tint (`#FDF2F4`) with deep rose typography (`#C26377`), `font-size: 11px`, `font-weight: 600`.

### Cards & Product Modules
- **Product Card:** Pure white background, `24px` rounded corners, no hard border, subtle ambient shadow (`0 10px 24px rgba(74, 62, 78, 0.05)`). Photography maintains a soft aspect ratio (4:5) with gentle inner radius.
- **Gift Box Bundle Card:** Tinted in soft cream (`#F8EDE3`) with a dotted border accent in `#BCA5D8` to evoke kraft stationery and packaging craft.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Text Inputs:** Pill-contoured or `16px` rounded fields with `#FDFBF9` background, inset `1.5px` border in `#E5DCF0`. On focus, transitions cleanly to a `#9A80BD` ring with a 3px diffused outer halo (`rgba(154, 128, 189, 0.15)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Smooth rounded squares (`6px` radius) and circles with `#EAA2B1` checkmark fill and white glyphs.

### Lists & Accordions
- **Editorial Accordion (FAQs & Shipping):** Borderless rows separated by soft `#F8EDE3` horizontal strips. Accordion toggles feature circular buttons with smooth pastel rotation indicators.

### Specialty Boutique Components
- **Gift Note Ribbon Box:** A dedicated text-area container stylized with a subtle stitched edge pattern and a soft rose bow icon accent, inviting customers to customize their personalized gift notes.
- **Verse & Quote Banner:** Minimalist, centered display component using creamy parchment background tones, framed with gentle whitespace to spotlight uplifting quotes, scriptures, or artisanal origins.
